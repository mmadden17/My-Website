Gameplay

The gameplay of the Halo games is mainly focused on the sandbox. The mechanics are simple and intuitive, but the complexity of the games is found in how these mechanics interact with the sandbox. For example, there may not be any double jump mechanics, but you can throw a grenade below your feet to gain more height than a regular jump. The AI for the enemies also adds to this sandbox experience. The grunts will scatter if their Elite squad leader is killed. Interactions like these make every encounter memorable.

Setting

Halo's emphasis on the environment makes every level feel unique. With a variety of biomes and tones across levels, the game's setting is a big reason why I love it. The game masterfully envokes a mysterious tone while exploring the ring.

Multiplayer

Halo's many social features make it a staple for co-op and pvp multiplayer gaming. Playing through the campaign in splitscreen with a friend makes it even more memorable and fun. The online multiplayer pioneered the space with features such as forge.
